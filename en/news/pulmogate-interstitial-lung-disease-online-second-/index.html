PulmoGate, a startup originating from Nagoya University, has completed a seed funding round of approximately ¥330 million to develop an online second opinion service for interstitial lung disease (ILD). The funding reflects growing momentum in digital health solutions targeting rare disease management, combining AI-assisted diagnosis with remote healthcare delivery to reshape the diagnostic landscape for complex pulmonary conditions.

Interstitial lung disease presents significant diagnostic challenges, with marked variations in diagnostic accuracy across medical institutions. PulmoGate’s platform aims to enable patients nationwide access to specialist second opinions regardless of geographic location, while AI technology enhances the objectivity of radiological interpretation. As hospitals increasingly adopt AI chatbots for operational efficiency—with documented success in handling up to 80 percent of routine inquiries—specialized diagnostic AI tools for rare disease domains are emerging as the next frontier in healthcare AI deployment.

The capital infusion will accelerate platform development and strengthen clinical validation infrastructure through collaboration with pulmonary specialists. Given the limited patient population in rare disease domains, remote diagnostic support at a national scale offers substantial potential to address healthcare disparities and improve access equity.

PulmoGate targets commercial service launch by mid-2027. As large-scale biodata processing and AI integration drive precision medicine advancement, the company’s deployment of digital health solutions in rare disease specialties is positioned to become a significant catalyst for broader healthcare innovation momentum.
