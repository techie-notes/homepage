The platform is designed to assist physicians and healthcare professionals in making evidence-based clinical decisions and delivering patient care.

UpToDate Expert AI combines Wolters Kluwer’s extensive medical information database with generative AI capabilities to support complex diagnostic and treatment decisions at the point of care. The platform enables physicians to rapidly access precise, evidence-based information, a capability that responds directly to clinician demand.
