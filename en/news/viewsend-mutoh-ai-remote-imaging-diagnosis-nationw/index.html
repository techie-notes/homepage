ViewSend ICT and Mutoh have announced a business partnership to deploy an AI-powered remote imaging diagnosis support service across medical institutions nationwide. The initiative integrates generative AI technology to automate diagnostic support operations and streamline collaboration between healthcare facilities.

The service enables automated generation of diagnostic support materials in response to imaging requests from remote medical institutions. By automating processes that previously required manual handling by specialist physicians, the service is expected to reduce diagnosis turnaround time and alleviate burdens on medical staff. Notably, the automated initial screening function in image analysis is anticipated to significantly enhance operational efficiency for healthcare professionals.

Rural medical institutions face the challenge of limited specialist physician availability, but this service addresses that constraint by providing access to advanced diagnostic support from remote locations. The combination of interinstitutional networking and automation technology is positioned as an effective means to narrow the healthcare disparity between urban and rural areas.

Moving forward, both companies plan to accelerate nationwide rollout while exploring further advances in AI capabilities and customization tailored to individual medical institution needs. As digital transformation and automation implementation accelerate across the healthcare industry, this partnership represents a significant milestone in the remote diagnostic support sector.
