Fujitsu Launches AI-Powered Discharge Prediction System with Automated Transport Booking in Kawasaki

November 24, 2026Fujitsu Global
SOURCEFujitsu Global
STATUSResearch Stage
EVIDENCECompany Announcement
UPDATED11/24/2026

Fujitsu has initiated a proof-of-concept trial in Kawasaki, in collaboration with the city’s municipal government, to deploy an AI-driven system that predicts patient discharge times and automatically reserves transportation options. The system leverages machine learning to forecast when patients will depart medical facilities, thereby improving accessibility to taxi and public transit services and reducing wait times associated with hospital visits.

The platform ingests multiple data points—including appointment schedules, medical departments, and historical patient records—into its predictive model to estimate actual discharge times with high accuracy. Once a discharge time is predicted, transportation reservations are automatically confirmed according to patient preferences, minimizing idle time and missed departure windows. Fujitsu’s prediction engine incorporates variables such as weather conditions and time-of-day traffic patterns to refine its forecasting capability.

Initial trials at collaborating medical facilities in Kawasaki have demonstrated measurable improvements in patient mobility satisfaction, with particularly strong feedback from elderly and mobility-restricted populations. Transport operators also report enhanced demand forecasting accuracy, leading to improved fleet allocation efficiency and operational optimization.

The integration of patient experience enhancement with regional transportation services is gaining recognition as a critical component of next-generation healthcare ecosystems. Following the conclusion of the trial phase, Fujitsu plans to explore broader deployment across additional hospitals and municipalities, signaling accelerated momentum toward patient-centric digital healthcare infrastructure.
