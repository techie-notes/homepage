PreMed has launched Galapagos, an AI-native appointment system designed for healthcare providers that consolidates patient-facing booking and backend administrative operations into a single intelligent platform. Patients can complete appointments entirely through LINE chat, while healthcare staff can execute granular system configurations through an AI agent interface. This end-to-end integration addresses long-standing pain points in traditional appointment systems by automating both customer interaction and internal operations through artificial intelligence.

The system’s primary innovation lies in leveraging LINE, an already-familiar communication channel for patients, as the primary booking interface. Patients navigate the appointment process through natural language conversation, selecting specialties and confirming times without leaving the chat application. On the provider side, an AI agent manages administrative functions including workflow customization and policy adjustments that previously required manual staff intervention. This dual-layer automation frees healthcare personnel to focus on patient care while reducing operational friction.

PreMed’s solution differentiates itself by simultaneously addressing user experience and administrative efficiency rather than optimizing one dimension at the expense of the other.

Appointment systems serve as critical frontline infrastructure in healthcare delivery, directly affecting both patient satisfaction and staff workload. The emergence of AI-native platforms like Galapagos signals a broader industry transition from efficiency-focused digitization toward patient-centric experience design. As adoption accelerates, competing vendors are likely to introduce similar integrated AI approaches to capture market share in this essential healthcare function.
