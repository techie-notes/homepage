Plaza Create and Fast Doctor have announced a business partnership to transform private meeting booths into telemedicine access points. The initiative enables physicians from Fast Doctor to conduct online consultations lasting 15 minutes or less using the high-speed connectivity built into Plaza Create’s “Plaza Private Booths,” reimagining non-clinical spaces as functional healthcare settings.

The partnership’s innovation lies in repurposing an existing asset class — private office booths in commercial and workplace facilities — as healthcare infrastructure. Patients gain access to confidential consultation spaces without traveling to a clinic, while physicians can conduct efficient symptom assessments, diagnoses, and prescription management via videoconference. The 15-minute consultation window is tailored for initial evaluations and follow-up appointments.

The telemedicine landscape has recently expanded with platforms like LINE-integrated “march,” which provides end-to-end patient acquisition and clinical workflow support, and multi-language services such as “GLOBE Online Doctor” for international patient populations. The Plaza Create partnership represents a complementary approach: leveraging existing commercial infrastructure as a foundation for distributed clinical delivery, rather than building medical facilities from scratch.

As the partnership scales across multiple locations, it is expected to improve healthcare accessibility in urban areas while simultaneously decentralizing care delivery infrastructure. By designating non-medical spaces as healthcare portals, this model introduces a novel use case for digital health implementation with potential industry-wide implications.
