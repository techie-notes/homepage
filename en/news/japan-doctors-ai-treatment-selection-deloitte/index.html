A Deloitte survey has revealed that 51% of Japanese physicians are leveraging generative AI in treatment selection decisions, marking a critical milestone in AI implementation across the healthcare sector. The adoption level has surpassed the 50% threshold, signaling substantial transformation in clinical decision-making processes.

In preventive medicine, AI technologies are now being deployed to analyze health checkup data and predict the probability of lifestyle disease onset within 5 to 15 years. This shift from qualitative risk assessment to quantitative, high-precision prediction has the potential to dramatically improve preventive medicine outcomes. Concurrently, ViewSend ICT and Mutoh are expanding nationwide a remote image diagnostic support service incorporating auto-generation AI technology, accelerating inter-institutional medical collaboration through AI and automation.

Online services are expanding into specialized ophthalmology treatment. Neumee has launched telemedicine services for eyelid ptosis treatment with “Upneeq Mini Ophthalmic Solution 0.1%,” enhancing accessibility to specialized eye disease treatment that previously required in-person clinic visits.

New initiatives to improve healthcare access in remote areas are gaining traction. Utilizing postal services and other regional infrastructure for telemedicine expansion is advancing efforts to bridge rural-urban healthcare disparities. The convergence of AI technology and digital health is expected to enhance both the quality and availability of healthcare across Japan.
