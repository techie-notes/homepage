Vigilance AI, a new artificial intelligence platform designed to accelerate pharmaceutical and medical device safety compliance, has begun operations. The service automates the evaluation of safety information and generation of regulatory reports required by health authorities worldwide, reducing the administrative burden on pharmaceutical and medical device companies while improving accuracy and consistency.

Manufacturers of pharmaceuticals and medical devices face stringent requirements to collect, assess, and submit safety data—including adverse events and device malfunctions—to regulatory agencies in multiple jurisdictions. This process demands substantial data processing resources and expertise in navigating complex, jurisdiction-specific reporting standards. Vigilance AI leverages natural language processing and machine learning to streamline the entire workflow from safety data evaluation to automated report generation, addressing a persistent pain point across the industry.

The platform provider is now recruiting companies for proof-of-concept (PoC) trials to validate real-world performance and reliability. Pharmaceutical and medical device manufacturers are invited to participate in testing phases, allowing actual safety reporting workflows to inform the system’s refinement. Early-stage feedback from participating companies is essential for ensuring the AI meets the stringent accuracy and regulatory compliance standards demanded in this critical domain.

Automation of regulatory operations through AI promises to deliver simultaneous gains in compliance efficiency and reporting quality. As healthcare companies adopt such tools, response times to safety signals should accelerate, contributing to enhanced pharmaceutical and device safety oversight systems globally.
