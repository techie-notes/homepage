As AI applications expand rapidly, policymakers view regulatory frameworks as essential to ensuring healthy industry development, with draft regulations targeted for completion by end of 2026. The medical domain faces particular urgency given the rising prevalence of high-risk AI applications in diagnostic support and surgical assistance, necessitating clear assignment of business responsibility and robust patient protection systems.

Domestically, a new financing scheme has emerged in which five regional banks including Shizuoka Bank provide coordinated lending to support startups focused on AI-driven operational efficiency in clinical settings, advancing digital transformation across regional healthcare systems.

Japan is entering a phase where regulatory development, technology advancement, and business scaling support are advancing in tandem across the medical AI landscape. Robust funding mechanisms from regional financial institutions supporting emerging enterprises will play an increasingly critical role in accelerating healthcare transformation across less-populated areas.
