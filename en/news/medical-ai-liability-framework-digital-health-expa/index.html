Questions about liability when AI systems produce misdiagnoses, gaps between institutional accountability and AI performance warnings, and the urgent need for legal and ethical frameworks have become pressing issues. This automation wave is enabling healthcare professionals to redirect their efforts toward higher-value clinical tasks.

As healthcare demand intensifies globally, digital technology-enabled solutions have become essential infrastructure for sustainable healthcare delivery systems.
